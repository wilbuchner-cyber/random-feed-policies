Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains how the Random Feed Discord bot (the "Bot") handles information when used on a Discord server.

Information the Bot Accesses

When a user runs /feed, the Bot may read message history in channels configured by the server administrator. This can include message content, message and channel identifiers, attachment URLs, links, embed URLs, and related Discord message metadata. The Bot needs this access to find eligible media items.

How Information Is Used

The Bot uses the information it reads to select and share a random media item or link in response to the command. It posts the selected URL and, where available, a link to the original Discord message.

Storage and Retention

The Bot uses a local SQLite database to keep track of previously shared items, including message identifiers and URLs, so it can avoid repeating them until the available items have been used. When the cycle is exhausted, the used-item history is reset. The Bot does not intentionally maintain a permanent archive of complete message histories. Hosting, diagnostic, or platform logs may retain technical information according to the relevant provider's policies.

Sharing

The Bot does not sell personal information. Information is processed by Discord and by the infrastructure provider used to host the Bot as needed to operate the service. The Bot shares the selected content link in the Discord channel where the command was invoked.

Security and Deletion

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect the Bot's credentials and database. Server administrators can remove the Bot or restrict its channel access. To ask about deletion of stored Bot history or privacy matters, contact the email below. Removing the Bot does not delete messages or attachments from Discord.

Children

The Bot is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. Use of Discord remains subject to Discord's own age requirements and terms.
